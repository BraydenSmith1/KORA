## 1. Executive Summary

The distributed energy resource (DER) management software market is experiencing rapid growth, driven by the global energy transition, declining renewable costs, and increasing grid decentralization. Within this market, mini-grid and microgrid optimization software represents a high-growth segment with acute customer pain points and limited incumbent solutions.

### Key Findings

**Market Size:**
- Global DER management software: **€45 billion by 2030** (18% CAGR)
- Mini-grid/microgrid segment: **€8 billion by 2030** (32% CAGR)
- African mini-grid software: **€450 million by 2028** (45% CAGR)

**Market Dynamics:**
- 35,000+ operational mini-grids globally, projected 200,000+ by 2030
- 70-80% of mini-grids suffer significant curtailment (>40%)
- Software penetration currently <15%, projected 60%+ by 2030

**Opportunity:**
- First-mover advantage in optimization-focused software
- Hardware commoditization shifts value to software/intelligence
- Strong alignment with climate finance and impact investment

**KORA's Positioning:**
- Target initial market: €25 million (Madagascar + Kenya)
- Expand to: €450 million (Pan-African)
- Long-term: €8 billion (Global microgrids)

## 2. Market Definition & Scope

### 2.1 What is a Mini-grid/Microgrid?

A **mini-grid** (or microgrid) is a localized electrical system that can operate independently from the main grid, typically comprising:

- **Generation:** Solar PV, wind, small hydro, and/or diesel/gas backup
- **Storage:** Battery systems (increasingly lithium-ion)
- **Distribution:** Low/medium voltage network
- **Control:** Energy management and metering systems
- **Customers:** Residential, commercial, and institutional users

**Size Range:**
| Category | Capacity | Customers | Typical Use Case |
|----------|----------|-----------|------------------|
| Pico-grid | <1 kW | 1-5 | Single building |
| Nano-grid | 1-10 kW | 5-50 | Small village |
| Mini-grid | 10-1,000 kW | 50-5,000 | Village/town |
| Microgrid | 1-10 MW | 5,000-50,000 | Industrial/campus/island |

**KORA's Target:** Mini-grids (10-1,000 kW) and small microgrids (1-5 MW)

### 2.2 Market Scope

This study covers the **software and intelligence layer** for mini-grids, specifically:

**In Scope:**
- Energy management systems (EMS)
- Dispatch optimization software
- Demand response platforms
- Forecasting and analytics
- Remote monitoring and control
- Customer billing and metering integration

**Out of Scope:**
- Hardware (panels, batteries, inverters)
- Physical installation services
- Project development and financing
- Grid-scale DERMS (>10 MW systems)

## 5. Market Drivers & Trends

### 5.1 Primary Market Drivers

**1. Declining Renewable + Storage Costs**

| Technology | 2015 Cost | 2025 Cost | Change |
|------------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Solar PV | $0.30/W | $0.18/W | -40% |
| Li-ion batteries | $400/kWh | $130/kWh | -68% |
| Wind (small) | $0.25/W | $0.15/W | -40% |

*Lower hardware costs → more mini-grids → more software demand*

**2. Energy Access Mandates**

| Initiative | Target | Investment |
|------------|--------|------------|
| UN SDG 7 | Universal access by 2030 | $50B/year |
| African Union Agenda 2063 | Modern energy for all | €120B by 2030 |
| India Saubhagya | 100% electrification | $2.5B allocated |
| Indonesia PLN | Universal access by 2024 | $4.5B |

**3. Climate Finance Growth**

| Fund/Initiative | Mini-grid Allocation |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| Green Climate Fund | $800M+ to date |
| World Bank ESMAP | $500M mini-grid program |
| EU Global Gateway | €150B Africa infrastructure |
| AfDB Desert to Power | $20B Sahel solar |

**4. Grid Instability & Blackouts**

- South Africa: 200+ days of load-shedding (2023)
- Nigeria: <4 hours average grid supply in rural areas
- India: 6% distribution losses, frequent outages
- Result: C&I customers investing in backup/independence

**5. Digital Infrastructure Expansion**

- 4G coverage in Africa: 50% (2020) → 75% (2025)
- Satellite internet (Starlink, OneWeb) reaching remote areas
- Mobile money users: 500M+ in Africa
- Enables: Remote monitoring, automated billing, cloud optimization

### 5.2 Key Market Trends

**Trend 1: Shift from "Electrification" to "Optimization"**

Early mini-grid deployments focused on basic access. As the sector matures, operators recognize that:
- Curtailment is the primary economic challenge
- Revenue optimization is key to sustainability
- Software creates differentiation in commoditized hardware

**Trend 2: Consolidation Among Operators**

| Year | Top 10 Developers' Share | Trend |
|------|--------------------------|-------|
| 2020 | 25% | Fragmented |
| 2023 | 40% | Consolidating |
| 2026 | 55%+ | Concentrated |

*Implication: Fewer, larger customers with sophisticated needs → enterprise sales motion*

**Trend 3: Hybrid AC/DC Systems**

Traditional AC mini-grids being supplemented with DC microgrids for:
- Electric vehicles (e-mobility)
- Productive use (irrigation pumps, cold storage)
- Data centers and telecom towers

*Implication: More complex optimization required*

**Trend 4: Grid Interconnection**

Many mini-grids connecting to weak national grids:
- Buy power when grid available/cheap
- Sell back during outages
- Provide grid stabilization services

*Implication: New revenue streams from grid services*

**Trend 5: Productive Use Focus**

Donors and investors increasingly require:
- Support for income-generating activities
- Agricultural processing (milling, cold chain)
- Manufacturing (welding, workshops)
- Commercial services (internet cafes, phone charging)

*Implication: Time-of-use pricing and load management critical*

### 5.3 Technology Trends

| Trend | Description | KORA Relevance |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| AI/ML adoption | Predictive analytics mainstream | Core differentiator |
| Edge computing | Local processing, reduced latency | Architecture advantage |
| Digital twins | Virtual system modeling | Simulation capability |
